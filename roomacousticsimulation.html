🎛️ADAM A7V Specific Tips

Boundary EQ: The A7V has built-in HF and LF shelving trims. With ≥95cm from front wall, set the low-shelf trim to 0 or −2dB. If closer, use −4dB to tame bass buildup.

Tweeter dispersion: The A7V uses a ribbon-like ART tweeter with very wide horizontal dispersion — first reflections off side walls are very audible in a 2.9m wide room. Side-wall panels are non-negotiable.

Port output direction: A7V is front-ported — less sensitive to rear-wall distance than rear-ported monitors. Still keep ≥95cm from front wall for axial mode reasons.

SPL sweet spot: 85–90 dB at listening position. Avoid pushing above 95 dB reference in a room this small — modal resonance becomes much worse.

Narrow room caveat: 2.9m width produces an axial mode at 58 Hz (half wavelength). Every mix decision in the 50–80 Hz range will be unreliable without corner bass trapping. This is #1 priority.
